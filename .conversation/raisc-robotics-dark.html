About the club

Robotics is interdisciplinary by design. It brings together mechanical engineering, electronics, computer science and more to design, build and operate machines with control, sensory feedback and information processing.

Robots can work in dangerous environments, manufacturing processes and places where humans cannot survive. They can reproduce actions such as walking, lifting, speech and cognition, while many modern systems take inspiration from nature through bio-inspired robotics.

GKCIET’s Robotics Club was established to support the student community and campus innovation. Faculty and student members run tech talks and use their free time to build practical robotics projects. The club is coordinated by Dr. Habib Masum, Assistant Professor and Convenor (kingmasum@gmail.com), under the patronage of Director Prof. P. R. Alapati, with students working under mentor guidance.

In 2019, three club teams entered the AICTE–Chhatra Vishwakarma Awards with low-cost solutions for the challenge “How to enhance the income of a Village.” One team reached the Regional Convention at Heritage Institute of Technology, Kolkata, on 26 December 2019; another competed at the National Convention at AICTE Headquarters, New Delhi, on 24 February 2020.

What the club works on

Three connected areas, from hardware to intelligence to integration.

Robotics

Mechanical design, embedded systems and control — building the machines that sense and act in the physical world.

Artificial Intelligence

Applied machine learning and perception, from data to models that make sense of the world around a robot.

Intelligent Systems

Bringing sensing, decision-making and automation together into systems that work reliably end to end.

How the club runs

A mix of learning, building and competing, driven by members.

Workshops & hands-on sessions

Sessions on electronics, embedded programming and ML fundamentals, run by members for members.

Projects & prototyping

Small teams design, build and iterate on robots and intelligent-systems projects through the year.

Competitions & hackathons

Teams represent RAISC at robotics and AI competitions, applying what they've built under time pressure.

Research & reading groups

Regular discussion of papers and ideas in robotics and AI, keeping the club close to current research.
